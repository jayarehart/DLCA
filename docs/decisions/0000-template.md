# NNNN. Short title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decided by:** name (leave blank while Proposed)

## Context

What problem or question forced a choice? What do we know, and where does it
come from? Two to five sentences.

## Decision

What we will do, in plain words. Start with "We will ...".

## Alternatives considered

- **Alternative A.** Why it lost.
- **Alternative B.** Why it lost.

State at least one alternative. If the reason for rejecting it is a guess,
say so.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, and what this commits us to. Name any
follow-up work or limits.

## References

Issues, PRs, papers (with DOI), data sources, and related records.
